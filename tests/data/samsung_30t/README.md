# Samsung INR21700-30T test files

Processed from P. Kollmeyer and M. Skells, Samsung INR21700 30T 3Ah Li-ion Battery Data, Mendeley Data v2 (2023), doi:10.17632/9xyvy2njj3.2, licensed **CC BY 4.0**. The tests were performed at McMaster University by Dr. Phillip Kollmeyer.

Unchanged copies of the pulse (HPPC) test at each of 40, 25, 10, 0, -10 and -20 degC, and the C/20 discharge and charge at 25 degC: what `packtherm fit` fits the cell on, with the drive cycles and discharges in `validation/data/samsung_30t/`. Copied by `validation/prepare_mcmaster.py 30t`.
