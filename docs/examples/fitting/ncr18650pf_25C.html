The model has two branches, so two time scales besides the instant drop. A cell responds on more: the worst errors sit in the first tenth of a second after a step (faster than either branch), and slow creep over minutes in long pulses and rests is missed. A model with more branches (planned) can follow both. Below 20% state of charge the open-circuit voltage is steep, so small errors in the state of charge show up as larger voltage errors.
