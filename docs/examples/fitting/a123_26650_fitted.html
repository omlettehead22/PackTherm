The model has three branches besides the instant drop: seconds (charge transfer), tens of seconds and minutes. The worst errors sit in the first tenth of a second after a step and in the collapse near empty. Below 20% state of charge the open-circuit voltage is steep, so small errors in the state of charge show up as larger voltage errors. Long tests are scored down to the knee.

Open-circuit voltage

slow discharge at 0.0827 A over 31.2 h, plus its own drop (3.2 mV: 0.0827 A x 38.9 mOhm, the fitted model's steady resistance, rising near empty as the model's does). At the other temperatures, from their own slow discharges, on the same state of charge (the charge left, as a share of 2.578 Ah): a cell that delivers less there reaches its cut-off at a higher state of charge. Between them the voltage is interpolated in temperature.
