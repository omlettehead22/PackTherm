# LG M50: drive cycles of three cells

Data: M. Gulsoy, K. Kucuk, A. Barai, J. Marco (University of Warwick), "Dataset of internal temperature during
lithium-ion battery ageing", Mendeley Data v2, doi:10.17632/bf6fksxpp2.2, licensed CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Extracted and converted to CSV by validation/prepare_m50_drive.py; averaged into 1 s bins (mean current, last voltage); otherwise no values changed other than the current's sign (positive on discharge), the time origin and the part kept.
